## Game media, charts and artwork

Card art, thumbnails, jackets, character names, music, SUS charts and game data remain the property of SEGA, Colorful Palette, Crypton Future Media and the respective rights holders. The program's GPL license does not relicense these assets.

Default CN public mirror: <https://storage.exmeaning.com/sekai-cn-assets/>. The resource path structure is cross-referenced from Moesekai's primary `web/src/lib/assets.ts` and verified against actual CN resources.

Local samples and their exact source URLs are listed in `data/charts/asset-sources.json` and `data/charts/sources.json`. Two local sample audio files are size-reduced MP3 copies, with original timing and leading silence preserved. The six included SUS files are genuine CN files and are not generated demonstration charts.

The image compositor uses the original card art together with Yoisaki vector/text layout. `examples/Team-Report.png` is a report generated by the actual application using an explicitly labelled editable example team; it is not a screenshot of a real user's account.
